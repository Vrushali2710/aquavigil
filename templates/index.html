1. Watch the system: AquaVigil looks at pressure, flow, and valve activity to spot unusual changes in the water network.

2. Compare the location: It checks whether the affected area sits near a vulnerable pipe corridor or a likely contamination risk.

3. Explain the risk: It turns the data into a clear score and a plain-language recommendation for residents or utility teams.

AquaVigil takes raw operational signals and simplifies them into understandable actions. Instead of showing only numbers, it explains what the pattern means and what should happen next.

How the filter image is analyzed: the system reviews the canister photo for signs like discoloration, buildup, sediment, and texture change.

What it does next: it converts those visual clues into a fouling estimate, then combines that estimate with the local pipe condition and risk context.

Why this matters: the result helps determine whether the filter problem is isolated or part of a larger utility risk pattern.
