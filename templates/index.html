Q1

How does this work without complete utility maps?

Defense: AquaVigil operates effectively on sparse, incremental data. Street-level pipe corridors follow public road easements readily extracted from OpenStreetMap. Pairing known pump shutoff schedules with public road elevation models establishes baseline drain-down risk before integrating subsurface sewer surveys.

Q2

Can a smartphone photo trigger a public boil-water alert?

Defense: No. A single photograph cannot establish microbiological contamination. AquaVigil uses a strict two-tier escalation architecture: Tier 1 flags internal infrastructure corridors for utility inspection; Tier 2 issues precautionary maintenance guidance to households and escalates high-risk cases for utility operational verification. Official public health notices require corroborating operational evidence.

Q3

Can Gemini distinguish standard rust from organic biofilm?

Defense: Gemini analyzes surface texture, optical opacity, and coating uniformity rather than raw color alone, flagging visual indicators consistent with possible biofilm or organic deposition as an inference rather than a laboratory microbiological assay. The visual fouling score is combined with the BigQuery spatial-risk score to produce a contextual escalation score.
